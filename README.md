#  PrepPulse AI

**PrepPulse AI** is an interactive, intelligent study workspace designed to transform raw notes, lecture PDFs, and resumes into an active learning engine. Featuring a warm, organic color palette—composed of nude browns, soft creams, warm greys, and earthy sage greens—PrepPulse AI creates a calm, focused environment for mastering complex technical subjects and preparing for interviews.

---

##  Key Features

*  **Notes Hub**: Upload lecture notes, slides, or resumes (PDF/TXT) to instantly parse and populate a searchable, active knowledge base.
*  **Interactive Flashcards**: Engage in active recall with 3D card-flipping decks equipped with status tracking (*Review Later* / *Mastered*).
*  **Adaptive Quiz Engine**: Take targeted multiple-choice quizzes with dynamic scoring, instant validation, and thorough explanations for every question.
*  **AI Mock Interviewer**: Practice technical interviews out loud or via text with real-time response streaming and instant evaluation across *Clarity*, *Technical Depth*, and *Actionability*.
*  **Analytics Dashboard**: Monitor study streaks, track overall deck mastery percentages, and view AI-recommended next steps tailored to your performance gaps.

##  Color Palette & Design System

| Token | Hex Code | Role |
